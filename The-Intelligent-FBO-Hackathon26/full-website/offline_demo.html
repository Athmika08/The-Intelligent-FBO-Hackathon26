03 / HOW IT WORKS

Built on transparent assumptions

Our forecasting prototype makes its inputs, limitations, and human responsibilities explicit.

01

Baseline activity

Four weeks of synthetic arrivals are summarized into average counts by weekday and two-hour interval.

baseline_analysis.py → baseline.csv
02

Event adjustments

Historical Super Bowl aviation reports inform assumed demand multipliers for the seven-day event window.

03

Scenario planning

Multipliers are applied to baseline counts. Demand is compared against simulated ground-service capacity.

SAFETY & UNCERTAINTY

Humans remain in command

Not a live traffic feed. Arrivals and event-day multipliers are synthetic assumptions; the actual 2027 result is unknown.

Airport-wide historical ratios do not directly measure arrivals at a single FBO. Low/Medium/High are planning cases, not confidence intervals.

This tool must never independently authorize aircraft landings, ground movements, or changes to safety procedures.
